## Root Causes (4 Issues Identified)

### 1. **NLV Calculation Algorithm Bug** (Lines 62-74, analytics_service.py)
- **Problem:** Local NLV calculation includes uninvested cash as part of position equity
- **Impact:** Inflates NLV by ~$200+
- **Fix:** Use eToro Account.total_equity as authoritative source instead of recalculating

### 2. **P&L Formula Error** (Lines 110-111, dashboard_service.py)
- **Problem:** P&L calculated as `(NLV - Invested_Capital)` instead of `(Unrealized + Realized)`
- **Impact:** Double-counts cash, inflates P&L by $1,146+
- **Fix:** Implement position-based P&L calculation

### 3. **Invested Capital Misdefinition** (transaction_repository.py)
- **Problem:** Invested_Capital includes uninvested cash balance
- **Impact:** ROI calculation becomes meaningless
- **Fix:** Track only position entry costs, not total deposits

### 4. **No Data Authority Tracking** (etoro_service.py + dashboard_service.py)
- **Problem:** No sync timestamps or source flags for metrics
- **Impact:** Can't determine which data is authoritative
- **Fix:** Add `last_portfolio_fetch_time` tracking and `data_source` field
